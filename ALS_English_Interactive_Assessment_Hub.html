How to use this hub

  1. Choose the lesson assigned by your ALS teacher.
  2. Enter the learner information requested in the lesson.
  3. Read the lesson and complete the activities.
  4. Go to the Assessment section and answer all 10 questions.
  5. Submit your answers and review your score and feedback.
  6. Use the lesson’s progress or record feature, if available, to save your work before closing the page.

The lessons open as separate pages. Use your browser’s Back button or return to this hub to choose the other lesson.
